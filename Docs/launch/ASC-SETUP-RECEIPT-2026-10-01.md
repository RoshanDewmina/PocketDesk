### Description — saved

See your Mac's screen on your iPhone or iPad and control it with a touch. Move the pointer, click, scroll, type and zoom, then get back to what you were doing.

SET UP ONCE
Install the free companion on your Mac and give it the two permissions macOS asks for. Scan the code it shows, then approve your phone on the Mac. There is no Farside account to create and nothing to change on your router.

THE SCREEN IS YOUR TRACKPAD
Slide a finger anywhere to move the pointer. Tap to click, use two fingers to scroll, and press and drag to select. Right-click and double-click are one gesture away. On supported iPhones, clicks come with haptic feedback. A short practice pad teaches the gestures without touching your Mac.

READ SMALL TEXT
Pinch to zoom in and the view follows your pointer, so a terminal line or a spreadsheet cell stays readable on a phone. Switch between Fit and Fill, and use portrait or landscape.

REAL KEYS
Type with the iPhone keyboard plus a key bar for Command, Option, Control, Shift, Tab, Esc and the arrow keys. Paste text to your Mac or copy text from it when you choose.

TALK INSTEAD OF TYPING
Tap the microphone, speak, then tap Done. Your words are typed on your Mac.

VIEW OR CONTROL
Just want to look? Switch to View mode and your touches stay on the phone.

YOU STAY IN CHARGE ON THE MAC
The companion lives in your menu bar. It shows when a phone is connected and who is steering. You approve every phone. Turn off control to allow viewing only, pause sharing for ten minutes, or stop sharing with one click.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your screen travels encrypted between your devices, directly or through a relay. Farside does not record your screen, keystrokes or voice. No ads.

FREE ON YOUR LOCAL NETWORK
At home or in the office, on the same network as your Mac, Farside is free with no time limit.

FARSIDE ANYWHERE
Farside Anywhere is an optional auto-renewing subscription, available monthly or yearly, that connects your phone to your Mac over cellular or any other network, with no VPN or port forwarding. Eligible new subscribers may receive a free trial. The price and terms are shown in the app before you subscribe.
