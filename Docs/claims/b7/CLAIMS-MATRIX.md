## Method, statuses and evidence provenance

- **PROVEN**: the stated scope has direct evidence. “PROVEN (source only)” never means release-device acceptance.
- **PROVEN-ON-OLD-BUILD**: evidence exists, but on a different/unclear historical binary. Recheck on the uploaded binary where the gate requires it.
- **UNTESTED**: source may exist; appropriate evidence for the complete claim is absent.
- **FAILS**: an applicable observed check fails. A historical failure is explicitly dated and cannot establish a present failure without rerun.
- **NOT TRUE**: exact wording is contradicted by this baseline/source or overstates what exists.
- **AUTO**: source/static inspection or automated fixture/core evidence. Static inspection is explicitly identified; test file existence is not a passing test receipt.
- **SIM**: simulator UI/layout evidence. It cannot prove haptic feel, live Mac input, physical folding, or actual speech recognition.
- **HANDS**: physical phone/Mac/iPad task, release-device acceptance, or owner/provider/store confirmation where applicable. Store/provider gates are identified separately; they are not simulator tests.
