Farside puts the Mac you already own in your hand, designed for touch first.

Use your iPhone screen as your Mac's trackpad: slide to move the pointer and tap to click.

There is no Farside account. The free Mac companion shows a code; scan it and approve the phone on the Mac before controlling it.

Farside is free on the same Wi-Fi. Farside Anywhere, an optional subscription for use away from home, is coming soon. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later.

Farside 1.0 is planned for iPhone and iPad.
