Farside is made by one person: I'm Roshan, a solo indie developer in Ontario, Canada. I built it around one rule: your Mac pairs only with a phone you approve on it, with no account to create. The iPhone app is native SwiftUI and UIKit; the Mac companion uses ScreenCaptureKit and VideoToolbox. Accessibility: I ran iPhone and iPad simulator screen audits; fixes and device checks remain. English at launch.
