Farside motion lab · brand

Twenty-four ways to move one idea. A fingertip on the glass, a pointer far away, and one ember dot where they meet. Everything here runs live. Press Replay, poke the stages, and keep what works for the website, the logo and the social cuts.

  • Ember means contact
  • Text sits on solid plates
  • Halftone art at 30 fps or less
  • Paused when off-screen
  • Reduce Motion gets a still

Scope: brand, website and social motion. In-app micro-interactions (connect, click ripples, the pointer, the dock, pairing, errors, Dynamic Island) have their own lab, so they don't appear here.

Reduce Motion is on, so every study shows its still frame. Replay plays one pass when you ask for it.

H2Orbit, then snap

The ember dot circles the pointer like it's making up its mind, then clicks into the tip. Contact is the payoff, so it lands with a press and a ripple.

Where it goes
Hero variantSplashOG loop 3 s
Build
Canvas 2D. The orbit is a tilted ellipse (ry = 0.42·rx) whose radius spirals out and decays; on the far half the dot draws behind the arrow. The snap is a closed-form spring (ζ 0.5, ω 19 rad/s) from the orbit's last position and velocity, so every replay is identical. Press 140 ms, ripple 0.9 s.
Video
After Effects: the dot on an elliptical path, then a spring expression on Position for the snap. Ember flash for 4 frames on contact.

H38,421 km

A wide, quiet opener. The pointer crosses a dotted globe to reach your Mac while the distance counts down. On arrival it says, deadpan, that this is close enough.

Where it goes
Hero variantRemote section16:9 social
Build
About 2,600 points on a Fibonacci sphere, orthographic projection, sized by depth; land is a 3D value-noise mask. The path is a great circle (slerp) lifted by 0.2·sin(πu), and the two ends really are 8,421 / 6,371 radians apart.

L2The dot on the i

The wordmark ships without the dot on its i. The ember dot drops in, bounces twice and settles, and the letters flinch when it lands.

Where it goes
Animated logoEnd card stingFavicon
Build
Gravity at 26 em/s², restitution 0.45, squash and stretch from velocity. A magnet (spring k 70) pulls within about 1.25 em of the i. On landing, neighbouring dots get a damped kick that falls off with distance. If the page has sound, one soft tock on the first impact.

One page breaks into dots, the dots trade places, and the next page sharpens back. It reads as the same brand texture instead of a generic fade.

Where it goes
Route changePoster to videoSocial transition
Build
Both pages are rendered once to low-res luma buffers. C1The pointer is the cursor

The site's own cursor is the Farside pointer. It trails with the app's camera ease, gets a halftone halo over anything clickable, and leaves an ember contact dot on click. The buttons press back.

Where it goes
Site-wide cursorMouse and trackpad onlyEvery button
Build
cursor: none only under (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine). A fixed SVG moves by transform in rAF with p += (target − p)·(1 − e^(−dt/0.075)), about 0.36 s to settle.
