## 0. Summary

1. **The biggest activation cliff is the device handoff.** A person who installs the phone app first cannot get the Mac app from the phone, and a person who installs the Mac app first cannot get the phone app from the Mac. [C] Comparable apps solve it with a share sheet or "send me a link", a short URL printed where the user is looking, "remind me later", a persistent "Get the desktop app" entry, and a download banner on the QR screen itself (Granola, Wispr Flow, Dropbox, Telegram, WhatsApp Web, Slack, Hilos). [S]
2. **Pairing is secure but not yet "it just works".** Today it is QR or pasted code, then Approve on the Mac. [C] The zero-friction upgrade is **nearby pairing over Bonjour** (Mac appears in a list, tap it, confirm a number, click Allow on the Mac) with QR and paste kept as fallbacks. Precedents: Roku, Sonos, Hue, Steam Link, KDE Connect, Apple's AirPods and Watch flows. It needs a security review because PRODUCT.md rules out weak short codes and requires an authenticated handshake. [I, C]
3. **The Mac wizard is already good** (live permission checks, Skip to watch-only, six-row ready check). [C] Its gaps: it "finishes" before anything real happens; the Pair step has a disabled Continue and no "later" path; the QR expires on screen instead of refreshing; and the Permissions row tells the user to look for **"PocketDesk Host"** in System Settings (a leaked pre-rename name). [C]
4. **Free tier = same Wi-Fi only (D28), so the iOS Local Network permission is required, not "faster".** The current priming says "Connect faster at home." [C] iOS gives no API to read that permission, so denial looks like a silent hang unless the app detects it by timeout. [S] Roku and Hue show the model for wording and for the "no devices found" state. [S]
5. **macOS permission handling has real traps.** `CGPreflightScreenCaptureAccess` can disagree with reality; a removed-and-re-added grant may not re-prompt until a reboot; Sequoia adds a periodic re-authorization prompt that matters for an unattended Mac; macOS 15+ also has a Local Network privacy switch for Mac apps; the macOS 26 pane is titled "Screen & System Audio Recording". [S] Use real capability probes and a repair ladder.
6. **A menu-bar-only Mac app can vanish.** On notched MacBooks the icon can be hidden with no overflow and no API to ask; Tailscale detects it via `occlusionState` and added a windowed app for exactly this reason. [S] Farside needs a window fallback (Dock, Spotlight, reopen).
7. **Multi-device is a product decision, not just UI.** One Mac pairs one phone today ("Pairing a different phone replaces this one"). [C] Second iPhone, iPad, second Mac and phone replacement need a small trusted-devices model.
8. **Mac entrance:** recommend a **≤3.8 s, once-only, skippable "Contact" sequence** (the icon's fingertip and pointer meet at the ember dot and the setup window develops from that point), Reduce Motion cross-fade, silent by default, bookended by the same motif at the first real connection. Dia's "spend the novelty budget in one or two high-value moments" principle argues for exactly that shape. [S, I]

## 1. Where Farside is today (from the screenshots)

**Phone, first screen** (`farside-phone-home-empty.png`) [C]
- Leads with the riddle "Your Mac is far. Your reach isn't." in large Doto, then a three-step instruction paragraph ("Install Farside on your Mac, choose Pair a phone in its menu bar, then scan the code it shows.").
- Primary button **Scan pairing code**, but a first-time user most likely has no Mac app yet. No "Get the Mac app", no "find my Mac", no try-first.
- Footer caption "FREE ON HOME WI-FI · ANYWHERE: OFF" is honest but jargon-adjacent.

**Phone, pairing** [C]
- Pair sheet with Scan / Paste Code; camera priming card with a single Continue ("Nothing is recorded. iOS asks next; choose Allow."); 3 numbered steps naming a two-minute window; inline expired-code error ("On your Mac, choose Pair a phone for a new one.").
- Local Network priming: "Connect *faster* at home", three rows, Continue, "You can change this later in Settings".

**Mac setup (4 steps)** [C]
- Hello: riddle H1, 3 cards, trust line, Continue. "STEP 1 OF 4 · ABOUT A MINUTE".
- Permissions: two rows, Open Settings buttons, live "WATCHING FOR THE SWITCH" state and GRANTED badge, Skip link ("your iPhone can only watch"). Continue disabled until granted. The Accessibility row says the entry "may be listed as PocketDesk Host".
- Pair: QR, three steps, "EXPIRES IN 1:43 · KEEP IT PRIVATE", Copy code instead. On expiry the QR becomes a "Code expired" tile with a New Code button. **Continue is disabled and there is no Skip.**
- Ready check: six rows (Screen Recording, Mouse and keyboard, A display to share, Connection, iPhone paired, Opens at login) plus Done. Opens at login is **off** by default. Done is enabled once checks pass; nothing proves a phone can reach the Mac.
- Replace flow: "Pair a new phone? Your current iPhone will stop working with this Mac." (single-phone model).
- Menu-bar popover already has a "Farside can't see this screen · Finish Setup…" state, which is a good recovery pattern.

**Repo facts** [C]: no Bonjour advertising or browsing in the code (grep for `NSBonjourServices`, `NWBrowser`, `_farside` found nothing). A local demo canvas exists (`PocketDesktop/DesktopPreview.swift`: "This desktop is rendered locally. It is not connected to your Mac.") and the phone already has a 5-lesson local gesture coach. PRODUCT.md constraints on how automatic pairing may be: expiring QR, explicit local approval, persistent revocable trust, **no weak short-code shortcut**, comparison code must be bound to an authenticated handshake and reviewed (F03, F04, line 396), and re-pairing after a lost identity happens beside the Mac, never remotely (line 421).

### Cheap wins (about a day each) [I]
1. Replace every user-visible "PocketDesk Host" string and confirm what System Settings will show for a fresh install.
2. Add **Skip, pair later** to the Mac Pair step (so Continue is never a dead end).
3. Auto-refresh the QR every two minutes while the pane is visible.
4. Reword Local Network priming to "needed to find your Mac".
5. Make Open at login an explicit recommended choice in the ready check.
6. Put **Get Farside for Mac** (share sheet) on the phone's empty Home.
7. Use the exact pane names: "Screen & System Audio Recording" and "Accessibility".
8. Give the replaced phone a plain "This Mac is paired with another phone" screen.

**macOS (Screen Recording and Accessibility)**
- Neither can be granted programmatically; the app must send the user to System Settings. [S/general]
- Naming: on macOS 26 the pane is titled **"Screen & System Audio Recording"**; macOS 26.1 requires an app bundle for an entry to show in that list. [Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/control-access-screen-system-audio-recording-mchld6aa7d23/mac), [Screenify](https://www.screenify.studio/blog/2026-04-23-macos-screen-recording-permissions) [S] Farside's copy should use the exact pane names.
- Detection: on macOS 26, one report shows capture working within seconds of the toggle with no app restart while `CGPreflightScreenCaptureAccess` still returned false. A real capture attempt is a better signal than the preflight flag. [issue #24](https://github.com/ThodsaphonSonthiphin/SPRecorder.Mac/issues/24) [S]

- Accessibility: `AXIsProcessTrustedWithOptions` prompts once per app identity; later calls can return false without UI; a background poll can detect the grant with no restart. [tile #22](https://github.com/filipmares/tile/issues/22) [S]
- Sequoia introduced a recurring screen-recording re-authorization (weekly in betas, later monthly). TidBITS notes many utilities are affected but does not address unattended remote-access apps, so the effect on a Mac you are away from is inference. [TidBITS](https://tidbits.com/2024/08/12/macos-15-sequoias-excessive-permissions-prompts-will-hurt-security/) [S, I] I did not find a source on whether this prompt still recurs on macOS 26 and 27; test it.

[Panic](https://help.panic.com/prompt/prompt-local-network/), [Michael Tsai](https://mjtsai.com/blog/2024/10/02/local-network-privacy-on-sequoia/), [forum 792453](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/792453) [S] Whether the Farside Mac host needs it for advertising or accepting LAN connections is unverified; test on a fresh install. [I]
- How comparable remote-control apps do it: **AnyDesk** has a "System Permission Status" window with one row per permission and a button that jumps to the right pane (older docs note Screen Recording changes need an app restart) [docs](https://support.anydesk.com/docs/security-permissions-on-macos); **Chrome Remote Desktop** walks through Accessibility and Screen Recording prompts after the installer; **Loom** tells users with a stuck grant to restart and run `tccutil reset All <bundle id>` in Terminal [KB](https://support.atlassian.com/loom/kb/mac-app-installation-reset-accessibility-permission). **Trust and security messaging patterns** [S unless noted]

**Timing and skippability guidance** [S]
- NN/g: most UI animations belong in 100–500 ms (simple feedback about 100 ms; 500 ms starts to feel like a drag); animations of a second or two are for unprompted changes the user should notice. [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-duration/) A first-run entrance is a deliberate one-time exception, so keep each sub-motion in that range and the whole under 4 s.
- Apple's onboarding guidance for games: avoid splash screens, menus, agreements and disclaimers when a new player opens the app, and let people skip introductory sequences. [App Store: Onboarding for games](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/onboarding-for-games/), [HIG Onboarding](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding) A general HIG principle: get people using the app quickly.

**Premium vs gimmicky** [I]
- Premium: short, tied to the product's own metaphor, plays once, leads straight into the first real step, and has a still-frame fallback.
- Gimmicky: cannot be skipped, replays every launch, adds sound at full volume, delays the permission ask, or animates fake progress (NN/g criticizes misleading progress bars).
- Farside-specific risk: a full-screen dim right before a Screen Recording ask can feel like a takeover. Keep it brief, show "ESC TO SKIP", never cover the menu bar.

## 4. Zero-friction pairing and adding devices (addendum A)

Roshan's requirement: "It just works" the first time on the Mac and when adding new devices.

### 4.1 Design rules [I]
1. The next action on the second device is obvious without reading (Find my Mac, Scan, Get the app).
2. Discovery is automatic when both devices are awake on the same Wi-Fi; scanning and typing are fallbacks, never the only path.
3. Trust is granted in one place, the Mac, with a confirm number, and instantly revocable.
4. Users never watch a code expire: invitations refresh silently; approvals wait with a plan.
5. Every failure says what happened, what to check and offers one button (Hue, Roku, Fitbit pattern).
6. Reconnection after months gets first-time quality (NN/g guideline 2).
7. Removal is one tap and ends sessions immediately.

### 4.2 What the comparables teach

| Approach | Used by | What "just works" means there | Cost for Farside |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proximity discovery, then one confirmation | AirPods, Watch, Sonos, Roku, Hue, Alexa | No codes, no typing | Needs Bonjour plus a sound pairing protocol and Local Network permission |
| Same-account auto-visibility | Continuity, AirDrop own devices, Astropad, Jump, Screens | New device just appears in a list | Farside has no accounts; a same-Apple-Account assist is possible (CloudKit) but adds a dependency and needs review |
| QR from the host or new device, scanned by the other | WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Tailscale, Brave, 1Password, Jump, Slack | One scan, then confirm | What Farside has today |
| Code shown on the target, typed on the phone | YouTube, Tubi, Steam Link, HomeKit | Works with no camera and no shared account; HomeKit uses an 8-digit setup code inside an SRP key exchange, then pins long-term keys [Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/security/communication-security-sec3a881ccb1/web) | A short code is only acceptable inside a PAKE with attempt limits; PRODUCT F04 forbids weak short codes, so this needs review |
| Compare numbers on both screens | Bluetooth numeric comparison, Signal safety numbers | A 6-digit number on both devices; a simple MITM shows different numbers with probability 0.999999 if the user compares [Bluetooth SIG](https://www.bluetooth.com/blog/bluetooth-pairing-part-4/) | No typing; needs an authenticated handshake behind it |
| Device list with revoke and inactivity expiry | Telegram, WhatsApp, Comet, Up, Chime | Users can see and undo | Farside has revoke in the spec but only one phone today |

### 4.3 Proposed pairing model (layered) [I]

**Layer 1: Nearby (default when on the same Wi-Fi).**
1. On the Mac the user chooses "Pair a phone". Only then does the Mac advertise a pairing service (for example `_farside-pair._tcp`; TXT record limited to a display name and protocol version, never a secret). No ambient advertising when not pairing.
2. On the phone, "Find my Mac" (after Local Network priming) browses and lists "Studio Mac" (Roku/Sonos pattern, with "Available on this network" and "I don't see my Mac").
3. Tap. Both devices run an authenticated key exchange. **Two variants for the security review:**
   - **A. Compare** (recommended for zero typing): both show a 6-digit number derived from the handshake transcript; the Mac says "Allow 'Roshan's iPhone'? Check it shows 472 913." and the user clicks **Allow on the Mac**.
   - **B. Code** (Steam Link, HomeKit, Tubi style): the Mac shows a 6-digit code that the phone types, used as a PAKE secret with a hard attempt limit. More robust against a hostile LAN, costs six taps.
4. Success: trust pinned, the Mac shows "Paired", the phone gets a success haptic and the ember ripple and lands on a Connect button.
5. The pairing window closes after two minutes and whenever the Mac's pairing UI closes. Rate-limit attempts.

Security flags: PRODUCT.md line 396 says a comparison code must be bound to a fresh authenticated handshake on both devices and reviewed; a cosmetic code is not enough. Nearby pairing needs the same independent review as QR, must never auto-approve by discovery or device name, and Bonjour will not cross guest-Wi-Fi isolation (KDE Connect docs; Hue and Shangri-La copy), so QR and paste must keep working. [C, I]

**Layer 2: QR / link (fallback and cross-network).**
- Make the QR encode a link that opens Farside when installed and the App Store page when not, that the system Camera app can scan (secret in the URL fragment so it is not sent to a server). Result: no in-app camera permission on the happy path, and one QR serves both "has the app" and "doesn't". [S for Camera behavior, I for design]
- Auto-refresh the invitation every two minutes while the pane is visible (each invitation still expires). Keep manual "New code" as a backstop, with a LINE-style note that the old code stops working.
- Have the Mac decide expiry (Amazon's clock-skew warning shows phone clocks can break time-based codes). [S, I]
- Constraint: Universal Links need a domain and an association file; there is no domain yet. [C] Until then a custom URL scheme works for installed users but cannot fall back to the App Store.

**Layer 3: Paste code.** Keep. Same-Apple-Account users get a free assist: "Copy code instead" on the Mac reaches the iPhone through Universal Clipboard when both devices meet Apple's requirements (same Apple Account, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on, Handoff on). [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102430) [S for requirements, I for benefit]

**Optional Layer 4 (later): same-Apple-Account assist.** A second iPhone or iPad on the same Apple Account learns that "Studio Mac" exists (for example through a private CloudKit record with no secrets) and lists it in Find my Mac even before it is on the same Wi-Fi. The Mac still requires one Allow. This is the Continuity feel without a Farside account, but it is a product and security decision. [I]

**Investigated and rejected** [S]
- **App Clip for pairing:** Bonjour is not available to App Clips, and physical invocations (QR codes) are capped at 15 MB on iOS 17+ (50 MB only for digital invocations). [Apple: choosing App Clip functionality](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/choosing-the-right-functionality-for-your-app-clip), [WWDC23](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10178/) The camera-scan-via-system-Camera route gets the same benefit without the limits.
- **DeviceDiscoveryUI system picker:** it is built on Wi-Fi Aware rather than Bonjour, so it does not fit LAN discovery of a Mac. [forum summary](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/804414)

### 4.4 Adding devices later

Decision needed: today one Mac holds one phone and pairing another replaces it. [C] Options:
- **Trusted-devices list (recommended).** A small cap (for example 3 to 5 phones/iPads) per Mac, one active session at a time, individual rename and remove. Matches Telegram and WhatsApp (four linked devices), Comet, and the Up/Chime lists with "current device", "last used" and "removed devices". [I]
- **One active phone with one-tap switch.** Simpler but keeps the "your old iPhone stops working" surprise.

| Case | Flow |
|---|---|
| **Second iPhone** | On the new phone: open Farside, Find my Mac. On the Mac: Allow "Roshan's iPhone 2". Both appear in Devices; one active session at a time. |
| **iPad** | Same app, same flow; iPad-specific coach copy only. |
| **Second Mac** | On the phone, Home, "Add another Mac" sheet with two paths: "I'm at that Mac" (Get Farside for Mac link, then Find my Mac) and "It already has Farside" (Find my Mac). The new Mac runs the same first-run flow. Show the Mac name large during pairing to avoid pairing the wrong one. |
| **Replace a phone (old one still works)** | New phone pairs as usual. The Mac's Devices list shows both; the user removes the old one. If capped, the Mac offers "Replace 'iPhone 14' (last used Tuesday)" by name. |
| **Lost or stolen phone** | Mac, Settings, Devices, Remove (or Remove all): revokes immediately and ends any session (F31, F12). [C] Show a "removed devices" history for reassurance (Chime pattern). |
| **New phone, away from the Mac** | Cannot re-pair remotely (PRODUCT line 421 [C]); say so plainly: "New phone? You'll need to be near your Mac once." |
| **Old phone after replacement** | Show "This Mac is paired with another phone" with a Pair again button instead of a generic unreachable error. |

Optional hygiene: WhatsApp logs linked devices out after 14 days of primary-phone inactivity and Telegram offers auto-termination of old sessions. For Farside, show "last used" and offer an opt-in "Remove devices I haven't used for 90 days". [S, I]

### 4.5 When something silently fails: detection and one fix

| What the user sees | Likely cause | How Farside can know | Fix offered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone spins on "Connecting" on home Wi-Fi | iOS Local Network denied (no status API) | No route after N seconds while on Wi-Fi; browse failure | Roku/Hue-style "Farside can't find your Mac" list with Open Settings and Try again |
| Phone: video black or frozen, Mac fine | Screen Recording stale, revoked or renewal prompt pending | Mac-side capture probe fails | Phone banner: "Your Mac needs a click. macOS is asking to keep Screen Recording on." Mac attention badge plus notification |
| Phone: taps do nothing | Accessibility revoked | Accessibility poll fails | "Control is off, view only. Turn on Accessibility on the Mac." |
| Never finds the Mac; both on Wi-Fi | Different subnet, guest network, AP isolation, or VPN | Bonjour empty and route fails | "Are both on the same Wi-Fi?" checklist with "VPN off" and "not already paired elsewhere"; offer Anywhere (paid) as an alternative |
| Phone waits after pairing tap | User did not click Allow on the Mac | Pairing state stays pending | Mac window jumps to the front, notification, chime; phone shows a picture of the Allow dialog, a timer, and after 20 s "Not there? Open Farside from the menu bar" |
| Old code or screenshot scanned | Expired invitation | Handshake rejects | Mac already refreshed; phone says "That code is old. Your Mac shows a fresh one: scan that." |
| Mac not reachable, fine yesterday | Asleep, locked, logged out, or app quit | Last-seen and error type | Existing "Your Mac is napping" copy; add "Farside isn't running on your Mac" when the host is quit; Open-at-login and keep-awake defaults |
| Everything broke after an update | Signing identity or path changed, grants dropped | Ready check runs silently on launch and fails | Menu-bar dot plus Finish Setup (exists); never reset grants automatically |
| Mac menu-bar icon not visible | Notch overflow, or "Allow in the Menu Bar" off | `occlusionState` not visible (cannot tell why) | One-time warning plus a windowed home; Dock notification for critical errors (Tailscale pattern) |

**Phone-side "Check my Mac" diagnostics** (Grab Diagnostics pattern) [I]: a list with green checks and at most one red row, showing only what the phone can honestly know: On Wi-Fi; Local Network allowed (inferred); Mac found; Mac awake; Farside running; and, once connected, what the host reports (Screen Recording, Control). One red row gets one button.

**Repair ladder for a stale Screen Recording grant (Mac)** [I, built on S]:

## 7. Mac first-launch entrance concepts

Shared rules (all options): plays **once, on the very first launch only**; replay via Settings ("Play intro again"); **skippable** with Esc, click or any key (jumps to the final frame with a 150 ms fade) and a faint mono caption "ESC TO SKIP" after 0.5 s; the setup window is interactive by about 2.8 s; VoiceOver announces "Welcome to Farside" immediately and the animation layer is hidden from accessibility; art runs at 30 fps or less and uses the still frame under Low Power Mode or Reduce Motion (design system rule 4). It must not run while a permission dialog is showing, and it never covers the menu bar. Each sub-motion stays within NN/g's 100–500 ms range or (for the big reveal) up to about 900 ms; the total stays under 4 s.

### Option 1 — "Contact" (recommended)
Idea: the app icon's two halves, halftone fingertip and crisp pointer, meet at the ember dot, and the window develops from that point. It echoes Arc's orb-reveals-window structure using Farside's own metaphor. [I]
Beats (about 3.8 s):
- 0.00 s: the desktop dims to about 88% black (350 ms fade; borderless overlay window per display, ignoring mouse events).
- 0.35 s: a sparse halftone dot field fades in.
- 0.60 s: the fingertip enters from lower left and the pointer from upper right, easing toward each other (primary ease, 900 ms).
- 1.60 s: contact. The ember dot ignites, one click-ripple expands. If a Force Touch trackpad is under the user's hand, a single subtle haptic tick fires (`NSHapticFeedbackManager`); otherwise silent.
- 1.70 to 2.60 s: the ripple's radius "develops" the setup window: dots resolve to crisp pixels from the centre outward. The overlay fades out under it.
- 2.20 s: wordmark `farside` cascades in per character (small rotate, blur and fade with a spring, 40 to 60 ms stagger, echoing Arc's text treatment). Tagline "Your Mac is far. Your reach isn't." holds under it for about 0.6 s.
- 3.00 to 3.60 s: wordmark settles into the Hello header position; step content staggers in (40 to 120 ms). Interactive by 2.8 s.
Sound: off by default. If Roshan wants audio: one soft one-shot "contact" tone of about 250 ms, not played when the Mac is muted. (No sourced information about Arc's or Dia's sound.)
Reduce Motion: no overlay, no travel, no ripple. 400 ms cross-fade from black to the Hello window, ember dot present and static.
Bookend: the same contact motif repeats, small, at the first real connection (ember ignites on the menu-bar icon), which is where Dia's "novelty budget" argument says to spend the rest. [S, I]
Risk: a full-screen dim before a Screen Recording ask can feel like a takeover. Mitigate with the visible skip cue, a fast fade and the once-only rule.

### Option 2 — "Closing the gap"
Beats (about 3.5 s): the desktop dims; a dotted line stretches edge to edge in the void; a Doto counter reads "GAP" and counts down to "0" (no fake distance number; the word GAP and the zero do the work); at 0 the ember dot ignites and the dots fold inward into the window. Lockup: "farside" then the riddle. Reduce Motion: static line with the counter at 0, cross-fade.
Pros: uses the brand's distance idea. Cons: a counter can read as a gimmick, and it tells a less physical story than fingertip meets cursor.

### Option 3 — "Develop" (quiet)
Beats (about 1.6 s): no overlay. The setup window appears as a halftone print and develops to crisp over 1.2 s, the ember dot pulses once, the wordmark fades in. Reduce Motion becomes a plain fade.
Pros: cheapest, safest, no takeover, doubles as the second-run and Reduce Motion version. Cons: least memorable.

**Recommendation:** ship **Option 1** for the first launch, with **Option 3** as the Reduce Motion, second-run and "Play intro again" variant. Build it after items 1 to 4 in the ranking; it is a brand moment, not an activation lever.

Handoff to setup: the last frame of every option is the Hello window fully rendered, so there is no extra click between the entrance and step 1. Arc and Dia both flow into long onboarding queues after their entrance (account, import); Farside's goes straight into "Control this Mac from your iPhone." [S, I]

Implementation notes (not verified against current Apple docs; check at build time): the dim layer is a borderless full-screen window per display; skip it when a full-screen app, Stage Manager or a multi-display setup makes it misbehave.

## 8. Things to avoid

- Gating the first minute with an account, an email capture, or a payment ask. Do not send the Mac link by email through a backend; use the share sheet. (Arc's forced account drew an exit reaction; Farside's "No accounts" is an asset.)
- Riddle-first headlines and jokes in button labels.
- Asking for camera, notifications or Local Network at first launch before the user's intent is clear (HIG: ask in context). Do not ask for notifications except when the user taps Remind me.
- Priming that behaves like a yes/no gate or a dark pattern. Keep a single Continue plus a real alternative.
- Overclaiming: "no cloud", "zero metadata", "private by design" (PRODUCT.md forbids these). Say only what is true: "We only look while a phone you approved is connected."
- Weak short pairing codes outside a PAKE, long-lived QR codes, auto-pairing on discovery alone, auto-approve by device name. Do not advertise a pairing service when not pairing.
- Assuming Bonjour works everywhere (guest Wi-Fi isolation, VPNs); always keep QR and paste working.
- App Clips or the DeviceDiscoveryUI picker for pairing (rejected in 4.3).
- Trusting `CGPreflightScreenCaptureAccess` alone; requiring an app relaunch when none is needed; auto-resetting permission grants; telling users to weaken macOS security or to run Terminal as the first step. Only user-initiated repair steps.
- Disabled Continue buttons with no explanation and no alternative.
- Showing internal names ("PocketDesk Host") anywhere the user sees them; using old pane names.
- Turning on Open at login silently.
- A menu-bar-only app with no window fallback.
- A full-screen intro that cannot be skipped, replays every launch, plays sound at full volume, or delays the permission step. Fake progress indicators.
- Showing the Anywhere paywall or review prompt before the first successful session.
- Copying Arc's length (about 28 screens across five flows). Farside's Mac flow is four steps; keep it that way.
- Dithering the streamed picture, text or controls (design system rule 2).
