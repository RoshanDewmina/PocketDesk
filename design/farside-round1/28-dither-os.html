EVERYTHING IS 1-BITEXCEPT THE BUTTONS

Farside puts your Mac on your iPhone and turns the screen into a trackpad. This is its design system: dithered pictures, dot-matrix numbers, and crisp controls wherever your thumb lands. Your Mac's screen is never dithered. We're dystopian, not monsters.

Signature: resolution lock

The one moment we animate on purpose. Everything else stays still.

When you tap Connect, your Mac arrives as noise and resolves in four steps, with one haptic tick per step. Then the dither steps aside and your real screen appears, sharp. The steps follow the connection, so the animation never adds a wait. Reduce Motion gets a single cross-fade.
