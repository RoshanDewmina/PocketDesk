Signature motion
  • Hero: four halftone CCTV feeds (office, kitchen, hallway, and viewer 01) run at a deliberately choppy 12 fps with scanlines, a rolling bar and tape noise.
  • At 1.3 s red brackets lock onto the Mac pointer ("pointer moving by itself"), at 2 s onto the phone in a beach chair, then a red string draws between them: "Same person · it's you". The pointer and the thumb then move together, and each click rings red.
  • Stats count up once. Live session: the pointer glides, the view eases after it, brackets lock where it stops.
  • Reduced motion: one still frame with the brackets and string already in place.
Rules
  • Red means "a phone is connected right now": REC dot, live label, End. Nothing else is red.
  • Halftone and time codes are backdrop; every sentence and button is IBM Plex Sans on a solid surface.
  • The joke always lands on you: no strangers, no faces, no creepy close-ups.
  • The hero canvas is a stand-in; a Higgsfield video can drop into the same slot.
