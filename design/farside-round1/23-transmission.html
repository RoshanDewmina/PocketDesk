THE IDEA

Your desk is a station; your phone is the receiver. Every wait becomes “searching”, every success becomes “received”, and every failure is a lost signal with a clear way back. Deadpan, a little dystopian, never confusing.

DITHER RULES

  • Canvas: Bayer 8×8 ordered dither, noise, phosphor persistence, sync roll.
  • Never on the live stream, text or controls.
  • Scanlines only over art, never over reading text.

OPEN QUESTIONS

  • The hero canvas can be swapped for a generated video; keep the lock-on beat.
  • Check green-on-black contrast in bright sun.
  • Sixtyfour for display only; body stays JetBrains Mono.
