How it works

Three steps. No account.

Nothing to sign up for. The pairing lives on your two devices and nowhere else.

1

Plant it on your Mac.

Install Farside. It lives in the menu bar and asks macOS for two permissions: see the screen, move the pointer.

2

Scan once.

Point your iPhone at the code, then click Allow on your Mac. That's the whole relationship.

3

Wander off.

Slide a finger and the pointer follows. Tap to click, two fingers to scroll, pinch to zoom. Swipe up for keyboard and voice.

Signature motion

The reach. In the hero, the phone in the wanderer's hand glows; a mote of light drifts across the meadow into the doorway‑window, becomes the pointer, and clicks. A stippled ripple rolls out through the grass and the status panel flips to “connected”. Everything is procedural canvas (ordered Bayer 8×8 dither, eight‑level ramp, live grain), so a generated hero video could replace the canvas later. With Reduce Motion on, you get one still frame with the pointer already inside the window.
