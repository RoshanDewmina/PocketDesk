Why this direction

It gives Farside a clear, fun metaphor people already understand: the phone is the controller, the Mac is the console. Buttons feel pressable, status reads like a HUD, and errors become "paused" instead of "failed".

Open questions

  • Tone down the scanline overlay in the real app (legibility, battery).
