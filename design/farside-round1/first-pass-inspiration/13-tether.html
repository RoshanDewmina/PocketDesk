How it works

Two knots and a tug. That's the whole setup.

Step 1 · on your Mac

Tie one end.

Install Farside on your Mac. It waits in the menu bar, quietly, like a good knot should. Two permissions and you're done.

Step 2 · on your iPhone

Tie the other.

Scan the code with your iPhone, then click Allow on your Mac. No account, no password to forget, no email from us. Ever.

Step 3 · anywhere

Tug.

Slide one finger and the pointer follows. Tap to click. Two fingers scroll, pinch zooms. Swipe up for the keyboard and your voice.
