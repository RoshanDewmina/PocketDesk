Signature
  • Hero cartoon draws itself in ink, then loops: thumb taps, a pink dot rides the dashed trail, the Mac pointer eases to Save, CLICK!, the cat wakes up.
  • Live session (screen 2) runs the real behaviour: pointer glides on a curve, the view auto-follows with easing, a halo marks the stop, and a click burst lands on the target.
  • Reduced motion: final frames only, no loops.
Rules for this direction
  • One pop colour, only for primary actions, live state and the pointer.
  • Ink outline is always 2.5–3 px; halftone dots stand in for shadow and depth.
  • Captions are quotes from the world of the panel; button labels are plain verbs.
  • All art is original ink work in this file, no borrowed characters or gags.
