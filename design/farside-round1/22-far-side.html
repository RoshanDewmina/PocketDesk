Signature motion
  • Hero: a live, Bayer-dithered moon (five levels, two inks, animated grain) turns slowly. At 1.2 s the tiny laptop on its dark side switches on, a ring of light ripples across the surface, and the status panel changes from Searching to In range. Clicking Download for Mac replays the ripple.
  • Your mouse is a torch: the far side lights up wherever you point. On phones the light drifts on its own.
  • Live session: the pointer glides on a curve, the view eases after it, a dotted amber ring settles where it stops, and a ripple marks each click.
  • Reduced motion: one still dithered frame, text shown without reveals.
Rules
  • Dither is material, never text: all words sit on solid or glass surfaces in Geist.
  • Sodium amber means live or lit (pointer, status, connected). UV violet is atmosphere only.
  • One italic serif word per headline.
  • The hero canvas is a stand-in: a Higgsfield video can drop into the same slot.
