How it works

Pair once. Leave.

No account, no cloud login. The pairing lives on your Mac and your iPhone, approved on the Mac.

1

Put it on your Mac.

Farside lives in the menu bar. It asks macOS for two things: to see the screen and to move the pointer.

2

Scan the code.

Point your iPhone at it, click Allow on your Mac. That's the entire relationship.

3

Get on the train.

Slide a finger and the pointer follows; tap to click; two fingers scroll; pinch zooms. Swipe up for keyboard and voice.

Signature motion

The tunnel. The night outside streams past; sodium lamps sweep across the passenger's face. Then the train enters a tunnel, the window goes black, and in the glass a reflection appears: their Mac's desktop, 812 km away. Their thumb moves; the reflected pointer follows and clicks Send; a ripple runs through the reflection and the status line reads “reconnected”. Procedural canvas only (RGB scene, Atkinson error diffusion to a seven‑ink duotone, live grain at ~20 fps), so a generated video could replace it later. With Reduce Motion on, a single still frame shows the reflection mid‑click.
