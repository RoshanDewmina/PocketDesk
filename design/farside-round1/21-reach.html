The idea

Two hands that can’t quite touch is the oldest picture of distance. Farside closes it: your fingertip on the glass, your Mac’s pointer on the far side, one ember dot where they meet. That dot becomes the mark, the icon tip, the live indicator and the click ripple.

Dither rules

  • Canvas halftone for heroes and art; CSS dot screens for UI texture.
  • Never dither the stream, text or controls.
  • Ember only for contact: live, click, stop.

Open questions

  • Canvas hero can be swapped for a Higgsfield video; keep the ember contact beat.
  • Tune halftone density for low-power mode.
  • Doto at large sizes only; UI stays Geist.
