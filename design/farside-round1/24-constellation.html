Rules for "just works"

  • Dither lives in pictures and backgrounds. Text always sits on a solid plate.
  • Your Mac's picture is never dithered. It's your screen, sharp.
  • Starlight marks one thing per screen: what's live or what's next.
  • Lines connect things that are actually connected. No decorative splines.
How it worksThree nodes, one lineSetupAbout a minute

Connect three dots. Then never think about it again.

1

Put Farside in your menu bar.

Allow Screen Recording and Accessibility, so your iPhone can see the screen and move the pointer. It sits up there like a small, very patient star.

2

Scan the code.

Point your iPhone at the QR code, then approve on the Mac. No account. There's nothing to sign up for, so there's nothing to forget.

3

Touch anywhere to drive.

One finger moves the pointer from where it is. Tap to click, two fingers to scroll, pinch to zoom. The view follows the pointer on its own.
