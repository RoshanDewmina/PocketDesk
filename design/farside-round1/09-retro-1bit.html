How it works

Three steps. Two apps. Zero accounts. Setup takes about as long as it took a floppy to format.

STEP_1.APP

STEP 1Put the helper on your Mac

It moves into your menu bar and stays quiet, like a good houseguest. Grant two permissions and it's ready. We point at exactly which switches to flip.

STEP_2.APP

STEP 2Scan the square

Point your iPhone at the QR code on your Mac, then approve it on the Mac. That's the pairing. No password, and no "please confirm your email".

STEP_3.APP

STEP 3Tap Connect. Steer.

Your screen becomes a trackpad. One finger points, a tap clicks (you'll feel it), two fingers scroll, pinch zooms. Swipe up for the keyboard and mic.

WHY THIS DIRECTION

1-bit makes Farside instantly recognisable in a sea of glass and gradients, and the constraint forces clarity: every state is black or white, on or off, awake or napping. Humour comes from system-dialog jokes and beeps rather than mascots.

OPEN QUESTIONS

  • Real app should keep the stream in full colour; 1-bit is chrome only.
  • Check Dither patterns at 3× on device for moiré.
  • Needs a high-contrast and Dynamic Type pass (pixel type at large sizes).
