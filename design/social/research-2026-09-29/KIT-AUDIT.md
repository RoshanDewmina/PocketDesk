I audited the whole kit: every video frame by frame, every still and carousel slide, the avatar, header and cheat sheet, and all the copy files. Nothing was changed or posted.

**Verdict:** it's on-brand and technically clean, but a stranger mostly can't tell what Farside does. No real product footage appears anywhere, the plain "control your Mac from your iPhone" line mostly shows up only in end cards and small footers, and every CTA assumes pre-launch ("Beta list · link in bio", "Coming soon").

## What the kit does well

- **Brand discipline is excellent.** It uses void, bone and a single ember accent, with ember only at points of contact. The mock home card and Connect button closely follow the real app's wording ("LAST REACHED 11:48 PM · WE WON'T ASK WHY", "CLOSES THE GAP").
- **Technical hygiene is solid [obs].**
  - All 7 vertical videos have the hook on screen at frame 0.
  - All 7 loop cleanly (last frame matches the first).
  - The safe-zone report is empty, cover PNGs are supplied, and every file is under 6 MB.
- **Headlines are big.** They're 118–150 px, so they read easily on a phone.
- **The voice works.** The deadpan tone is consistent, and it's honest about "beta" and "coming".

## Biggest gaps

1. **No real product anywhere.** No video shows a thumb on the phone moving the Mac's pointer with both devices in frame. v04 comes closest, with a generic outline at 5.3 s.
2. **Clarity at 2 seconds.** The use line lives in end cards (v01 from 7.45 s, v07 from 6.5 s) and in about 24 px footers on stills. Three of the seven videos (v01, v03, v05) repeat the same abstract hand-meets-pointer shot.
3. **The riddle tagline shows up without the plain line.** "Your Mac is far. Your reach isn't." appears in 4 video scripts, 4 posts, a bio option, the header and launch post 1/10 [obs]. Keep it only as a sign-off after a plain line.
4. **Small text.**
   - End-card footers measure about 17 px cap height in grey (#8C877F), roughly 8 pt on a phone [obs]. They carry key facts like "Free on the same Wi-Fi · no account".
   - The mock UI text in v04 is about 7 pt.
5. **All 9 videos are silent.** Buffer posts the file's own audio. As far as I know, adding in-app music means using its "notify me" mode and finishing in the app [inf, check Buffer]. TikTok's own guidance says sound on.
6. **Everything is framed for pre-launch.** Every end card says "Beta list · link in bio", p01 says "COMING SOON", and the cheat sheet says "don't state a launch date". All of that is wrong from Nov 17. "Link in bio" is also wrong for X and Shorts.
7. **End cards are too long.** v06 and v07 spend 24–34% of their runtime on the end card, which probably hurts watch-through [inf].
8. **Every platform gets the same thing.** Only 2 videos have 16:9 cuts. There's nothing for Shorts and no IG Stories. Threads gets only stills, even though the kit's own research says video engages best there.
9. **Minor brand drift.** Instrument Serif is used for whole sentences, not the one accent word the design system specifies. Four Instagram posts are 1:1.

## Where it feels "cody" and where it doesn't

- **Cody already:**
  - v06's terminal (`[y/n]` prompt and waiting timer), p05 and p10's build log.
  - p12's readout table and p06's crossed-out sign-up form.
  - The "GAP · 48 CM" readouts, tracked mono captions and window-title labels.
- **Not cody:**
  - v01, v03 and v05 read as poster art.
  - p02, p14, p15 and the c2 cover are brand illustration.
  - The whole-sentence serif punchlines read editorial.
- **How to push it:**
  - Terminal-style lower thirds, e.g. `$ farside connect "Studio Mac"` → `connected · direct`.
  - Key-cap glyphs like ⌘C / ⌘V.
  - Real app status text ("Your iPhone is steering", "Holding your Mac").
  - Real dev apps on the streamed screen (Terminal, Xcode, Cursor) instead of Notes and groceries.
- **One caveat:** the real Mac popover shows "DIRECT · 14 MS · 60 FPS", which breaks the kit's no-latency-claims rule. Either crop it or decide the rule doesn't apply to real UI.

## Per platform

- **X:** Text one-liners plus native video plus behind-the-scenes is the right mix, but it leads with the wrong asset.
  - Needs a real 20–40 s demo for launch post 1/10 and the pin.
  - Needs founder-account screen recordings.
  - Build around v06, p05 and p10; say "link below", not "link in bio".
- **Instagram:** Carousels plus 3 Reels a week is right, and the carousels are the best work in the kit.
  - Missing Stories: launch countdown and link stickers.
  - Missing 4:5 versions of the 1:1 posts.
  - Reels need sound and real hands.
  - Pin a real-footage Reel, c3 and c1, not v01 and p15.
- **Threads:** The conversation prompts are good. Add the videos and real screenshots.
- **TikTok:** The weakest fit. Polished, silent motion graphics go against TikTok's own advice (DIY, real people, sound on). It needs founder-on-camera, phone-in-hand clips 3–4 times a week; the calendar has only 2 "film yourself" slots.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Not covered at all. The 9:16 masters can be uploaded, but they need searchable titles ("Control your Mac from your iPhone") and no "link in bio" CTA [inf: links in Shorts aren't clickable, verify]. A 30–60 s demo suits Shorts [inf].
