New behaviour, kept small and built on existing actions (`HostModel`):
- **Pause 10 min** = Stop Sharing now, then Resume Sharing when an in-memory timer fires, unless the user resumes, stops or pairs first. After a relaunch during a pause sharing stays off, as after Stop Sharing.
- **Chime when a phone connects**, on by default (as in Reach), stored in `HostPreferences.chimeOnConnect`; plays the system "Glass" sound after a new connection authenticates and capture starts. Not played on a background resume.
- The view state now carries `accessibilitySkipped`, the timed pause, the parsed session readout, why the Mac is unavailable (locked, asleep, switched user, display asleep) and the name the bundle shows in Finder.

Capture, input, pairing, permission polling, keep-awake, lock/sleep handling, clipboard, pointer telemetry and the relay are unchanged apart from those hooks. Views follow `HostSettingsSection` (no `GroupBox`, no grouped `Form`).

## Follow-up: accent fonts and the shared halftone renderer

29 September 2026, branch `worktree-agent-a00c0691d236a622c` on `pocketdesk-remote-chat` at `0d9a13b`. Same limits as above: nothing installed, launched or signed; bundle identifier, `PRODUCT_NAME`, signing and installed path unchanged; the built `Info.plist` gains no keys.

**Fonts (open issue 1).** `Doto-Variable.ttf`, `InstrumentSerif-Italic.ttf` and `OFL-Doto.txt`, `OFL-InstrumentSerif.txt` are the phone's own files: `project.yml` lists `RemotePhone/Fonts` as a source of `PocketDeskRemoteHost` (and of `HostUISnapshotTests`), so there is one copy and all four land in `Contents/Resources`. They are registered in code, per process, by `HostFonts.registerBundledFonts()` (`CTFontManagerRegisterFontURLs`, the current form of the deprecated `CTFontManagerRegisterFontsForURL`; on macOS 27 it completes before it returns), not through `ATSApplicationFontsPath`:
- the host's Info.plist is generated from build settings, so a plist key would mean adding a hand-written plist to the signed app for something one call does;
- the two files are looked up by name, so a missing one fails registration (and the test) instead of silently falling back to SF;
- the unit tests run the same call; an Info.plist key only applies to the launched app and could not be tested.

`HostType` registers before it hands out a font, so launch order cannot matter. On macOS 27 CoreText names the variable font's instances as on iOS: `Doto-Black_ExtraBold` (display) and `InstrumentSerif-Italic` resolve; plain "Doto" resolves to Regular. The SF Semibold / New York stand-ins and the hand-drawn wordmark are gone (the wordmark is Doto at 20.8 pt, under the design system's 28 pt rule for Doto; it reads well in the Settings header). `HostHeading` keeps spaces in Doto and punctuation in SF, as the phone's `FarsideHeading` does, so the serif accent word gets the same gaps as the rest. `scripts/verify-remote.sh` now fails if the built host lacks the four files.

**Halftone (Halftone row).** `HostHalftoneArt.swift` is deleted. `HostArt.swift` holds the two scenes and `HostArt`, a thin wrapper around `FarsideHalftone`:
- the scenes keep the old compositions and the old 2 pt pitch and use the shared `FarsideArt.hand` and `pointer`; the glows are same-size dots thinned on the old 8 × 8 Bayer matrix, because dots that only change size end in a hard edge where they fall below the renderer's smallest dot, and they are drawn last because `FarsideArt.hand` multiplies everything drawn before it by its wrist fade;
- the live and calm popover strips move (a ripple along the signal, the ember breathing); paused and attention strips and the whole rail are one still frame (`HostArtScene.animated`, and a test pins that a scene animates exactly when its drawing depends on time). The rail is about 20 ms a frame at this pitch, more than a background utility should spend 30 times a second;
- animated art draws at most 30 frames a second (the renderer's timeline) and only while its window is on screen: `HostWindowVisibility` follows the window's occlusion state, because a `MenuBarExtra` popover is ordered out rather than removed, so `onDisappear` does not tell. Reduce Motion, Low Power Mode and a hidden window get the still frame, drawn at time 0, which is the old still art.
